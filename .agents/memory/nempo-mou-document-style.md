---
name: Nempo MOU document style
description: User direction for the visual structure and wording of Nempo MOU documents.
---

For MOU design, the user wants a professional, attractive document without unnecessary text or unclear numbering. The placement and arrangement of the content must be clear.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this direction for the MOU.

**How to apply:** Keep headings, clauses, party labels, signatures, and numbering easy to follow; remove decorative copy that does not help explain the agreement.

Keep page headers fully visible and large enough to read; allow long header text to wrap instead of clipping.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked that the MOU header not be cut off or too small.

**How to apply:** Check repeating headers on later pages in both preview and printed/PDF layouts, including narrow screens.

Downloaded MOU PDFs must keep text sharp, selectable, and unfragmented; do not rasterize the document or use excessive letter spacing.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for high-quality downloads with text that does not break apart.

**How to apply:** Preserve vector text and embedded fonts, then verify A4 dimensions, font embedding, and continuous text extraction from a generated PDF.

Keep each MOU page the same A4 size. When content does not fit, move complete sections to the next page rather than shrinking text or clipping it, and balance the page contents to reduce large empty areas.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for consistent A4 pages, overflow onto the next page when needed, and less unused space.

**How to apply:** Check page dimensions and pagination in both the preview and printed/PDF output after changing clause length or layout.

Include two side-by-side initials areas at the bottom of every page: one for Nempo and one for GGS Hair Studio. Keep the page number visible beside them.

**Why:** The user asked for a two-column initials area on every MOU sheet.

**How to apply:** Preserve the two party labels and a printable writing line on every A4 page, without overlapping the page number or clause text.

For MOU edits, preserve existing commercial terms and do not invent missing party identities, deliverable quantities, dates, or legal protections. Leave clear blanks or flag these items for the user to decide.

**Why:** The MOU had incomplete party details and no fixed monthly deliverable count; guessing could change the agreement.

**How to apply:** Improve wording and organization while keeping known prices and durations unchanged, and identify open deal terms before presenting the MOU as ready to sign.

For the Nempo–GGS MOU, the initial term is three months; any extension requires mutual written agreement based on whether the parties feel the collaboration is a good fit. Do not make it auto-renew.

**Why:** The user explicitly set this term.

**How to apply:** Keep the initial duration and optional, nonautomatic extension consistent across the term and renewal clauses.

For the Nempo–GGS MOU, the official name for the first party is Nempo.id.

**Why:** The user confirmed the official name.

**How to apply:** Use Nempo.id in the first party’s official-name field; keep “Nempo” as the brand name where appropriate.

For the GGS content schedule, GGS Beauty is the new women-focused brand with a grand opening on 18 October 2026; GGS Hair Studio is the established men-focused account.

**Why:** The user provided these brand audiences and the confirmed opening date.

**How to apply:** Give GGS Beauty a launch-led content plan and GGS Hair Studio an ongoing men's hair content plan; do not invent unconfirmed services, promotions, or contact details.

For separate Nempo–GGS content planning, the discussed 30-day period is 12 October–10 November 2026, with the grand opening on 18 October; omit social account handles.

**Why:** The user asked for the content to align with the updated MOU date and said Instagram and TikTok account names were not needed.

**How to apply:** Align any separately requested calendar with the agreed dates; do not add account-name placeholders or include the calendar in the MOU.

Do not include meterai boxes or instructions in the current Nempo–GGS MOU.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to remove meterai from this agreement.

**How to apply:** Keep the signature areas clear for signatures and dates, without stamp-related layout or copy.

Do not commit to a fixed number or daily frequency of content publications in the current Nempo–GGS MOU; content volume and timing remain adjustable by mutual agreement.

**Why:** The user asked for content quantities to be flexible rather than listed as monthly or per-platform counts.

**How to apply:** Remove numeric count fields and avoid a publication quota or daily obligation in the MOU.

Do not include a content calendar or appendix in the current Nempo–GGS MOU.

**Why:** The user specified that the content attachment is not part of the MOU.

**How to apply:** End the MOU after its signature page and remove any clause references to an appendix.

For the current Nempo–GGS MOU, the second party is GGS Beauty, while the purpose clause should continue to target promotion of GGS Hair Studio.

**Why:** The user supplied GGS Beauty as the second-party identity and explicitly chose to keep the purpose clause focused on GGS Hair Studio.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when editing the MOU; do not change the purpose clause to GGS Beauty or both brands unless the user asks.
