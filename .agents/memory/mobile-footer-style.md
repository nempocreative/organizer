---
name: Nempo mobile footer visual direction
description: Keeps the bottom navigation polished and legible without a cartoonish look.
---

Keep Nempo's mobile footer restrained and easy to scan: place line icons directly on the white dock with no individual icon tiles or backplates. Use a larger Nempo-blue active icon and dark-slate inactive icons. Avoid gradients, route-specific accent colors, multicolor illustrations, and pulsing decorations.

**Why:** The user rejected an overly colorful, cartoon-like footer, found small icons hard to see, then asked to remove the dark-looking icon shapes when active.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing navigation glyphs when restyling the footer. Keep icons large and high-contrast on the white dock; indicate the active item through scale and the single brand-blue color, not a background shape. This preference does not apply to the home-grid artwork.
