---
name: Digital services redesign review
description: Full-page visual redesigns need a section-by-section review after graduation, not just a hero screenshot.
---

For visual redesigns, compare the complete page after integration. A strong hero can hide old card treatments, spacing, or color systems that remain in lower sections.

**Why:** The first production pass of the digital services redesign visually changed the hero while lower sections still carried the previous rounded-card system.

**How to apply:** After graduating a mockup, inspect every major section and verify the production page against the approved direction before delivery.
