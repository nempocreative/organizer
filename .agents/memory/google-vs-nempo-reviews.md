---
name: Google vs Nempo reviews
description: Separation between external Google Maps ratings and first-party Nempo reviews
---

Google Maps ratings and links are external references, while comments submitted through Nempo are first-party reviews. They should never be combined into one average, count, or review list unless a deliberate data integration is added.

**Why:** The app does not have a stable public source for importing Google review text, and presenting the two sources as one would misrepresent the origin and meaning of the feedback.

**How to apply:** Label Google values as Google Maps, link users to the place listing for current external reviews, and keep the existing Nempo review component and aggregates separate.
