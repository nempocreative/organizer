---
name: Warm editorial visual direction
description: Shared visual direction for Nempo invitation, venue, and event partnership experiences.
---

Invitation and partnership pages should feel like real editorial experiences rather than generic SaaS screens: use warm neutral surfaces, strong photography, and restrained brand-color accents instead of abstract color blocks. Avoid AI-looking stacks of pills, floating badges, and glossy card grids; let one human story and one strong image lead.

**Why:** Feedback on the earlier partnership page found the oversized blue block too clean and visually monotonous.

**How to apply:** Keep the primary CTA prominent, let couple, venue, or event photography carry the visual weight, and reserve darker colors for readable type, image overlays, and small contrast moments. Prefer editorial composition, real photography, fewer decorative containers, and direct Indonesian copy.
