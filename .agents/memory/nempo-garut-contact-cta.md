description: Public admin and partnership contact CTAs use Instagram DM, while operational WhatsApp flows remain separate.

Public-facing admin, partnership, service, and general “hubungi” CTAs should route to the shared Nempo Instagram DM/profile contact. Operational WhatsApp flows—such as ticket delivery, buyer contact details, venue phone numbers, crew coordination, and social sharing—are separate and should not be changed automatically.

**Why:** Instagram DM is now the requested primary channel for contacting Nempo, but replacing operational WhatsApp workflows would break delivery and coordination behavior.

**How to apply:** Reuse the shared Instagram contact constants for new public CTAs; classify a WhatsApp link by its user-facing purpose before changing it.
