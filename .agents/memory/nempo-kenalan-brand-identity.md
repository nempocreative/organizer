Treat the Nempo Kenalan deck as a client-facing Nempo service presentation, not a community introduction. Its five services are creator/talent booking, marketing agency, creative production for businesses (content, visuals, branding), event organizer, and wedding organizer. Omit prices unless requested. Use official Nempo logo blue as primary, cyan/light-blue accents, and cool near-white/blue surfaces.

**Why:** The user clarified the actual services and confirmed that “kreatif usaha” means producing content, visuals, and branding for businesses; they also asked to align the deck with the logo and omit prices.

**How to apply:** Keep service claims within those five categories. Talent material supports MCs, photographers, videographers, content creators, and musicians. Use the Instagram DM CTA, and do not reintroduce digital software packages unless requested.

For client-facing Nempo decks, show client prices and package inclusions only when requested. Never include internal team costs, operating expenses, residual revenue, or margin calculations from a source proposal.

**Why:** Source proposals can contain internal cost and margin simulations that are not appropriate to share with prospective clients.

**How to apply:** When converting a priced or operational proposal into Nempo Kenalan slides, separate client-facing package rates from internal financial calculations and omit the latter.

Avoid adding a second slide that repeats a service already covered in the deck. Expand the existing service slide when more detail is needed; add a new slide only for distinct content.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected an additional Creator Booking slide because it duplicated the existing service slide.

**How to apply:** Check the slide manifest and current service slide before adding a slide. Prefer integrating booking details into the existing slide when they belong to the same service.

Creator Booking includes a team for Wedding Content Creator, Event Content Creator, and other event content needs when requested. It can also support client-event coverage and influencer video work.

**Why:** The user clarified Creator Booking use cases on 2026-10-22 and added Event Content Creator plus broader event coverage on 2026-10-30.

**How to apply:** Present these as Creator Team use cases in public service copy and client decks; do not imply they are separate Nempo services or create duplicate slides.

Keep Nempo Kenalan slides vertically balanced; do not bunch the content at the top and leave a large empty lower area. Give subsections, such as booking steps, a clear heading and enough spacing.

**Why:** The user called out a top-heavy Creator Booking slide with excessive empty space below.

**How to apply:** Review the full slide canvas, not only its top section, and distribute major content blocks through the available height.

The user supplied Creator Booking starting amounts: event moments Rp150,000, influencer video Rp150,000, and WCC Rp350,000. No billing unit was specified.

**Why:** The user provided the prices directly for the deck.

**How to apply:** Display these as “Start from” amounts without adding per-hour, per-day, or per-content units unless the user confirms them.

Marketing Agency works as an external marketing team for companies, covering strategy, marketing systems, team collaboration, and content; monthly contracts require at least 3 months. Keep client names out of the slide unless requested.

**Why:** The user clarified the service model and minimum contract term, then said client marketing examples are not needed on the slide.

**How to apply:** Position the offer as an ongoing monthly team partnership, not a one-off campaign. Explain in plain language what Nempo does as the company's marketing team, without naming clients.

Public Nempo messaging may invite Garut cafés to collaborate on events, highlighting the young creative team that can make a café feel more lively. Nempo Organizer handles weddings and other event types.

**Why:** The user stated this service positioning on 2026-10-30.

**How to apply:** Use these claims in public café-partnership and event-service copy without implying a specific event date or café partner.
