Nempo Cultural Tour is planned as a weekend gathering for Garut residents that combines an UMKM and local-food market, Sundanese/local cultural performances, acoustic and community entertainment, workshops, product demonstrations, and family activities. Proposed rotating themes are Rasa Lokal, Panggung Budaya, Pasar Kreatif, and Culture & Music.
