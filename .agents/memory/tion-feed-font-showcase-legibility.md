---
name: Tion Feed font showcase legibility
description: Keep font specimens readable in compact 4:5 recommendation posts.
---

In a compact two-column font guide, preserve a font's recognizable appearance and let long names wrap at a natural word boundary instead of forcing every name onto one line.

**Why:** Fonts have very different word widths; shrinking the longest specimen until it fits makes the examples harder to compare and read at phone size.

**How to apply:** Check both the full 4:5 artwork and a mobile preview, and use intentional line breaks for long font names.
